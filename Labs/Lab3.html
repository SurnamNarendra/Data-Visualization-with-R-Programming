Probability Distributions

Aim

To understand and simulate Normal, Binomial, and Poisson distributions in R.

Algorithm / Procedure

  • Use rnorm(), rbinom(), rpois() to generate random samples.
  • Plot histograms and overlay density curves.

Input / Output

Input:
> x <- rnorm(100, mean=0, sd=1)
> hist(x)
Output:
Histogram of Normal distribution

Result

Simulated and visualized key probability distributions.
