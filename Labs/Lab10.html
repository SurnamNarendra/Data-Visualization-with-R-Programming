ANOVA (Analysis of Variance)

Aim

To conduct one-way ANOVA and interpret group mean differences.

Algorithm / Procedure

  • Use aov() and summary() functions.
  • Plot results with boxplots.

Input / Output

Input:
> aov_result <- aov(Weight ~ Diet, data=data)
> summary(aov_result)

Result

Tested significance of group mean differences using ANOVA.
