Hypothesis Testing – t-test

Aim

To perform one-sample, two-sample, and paired t-tests in R.

Algorithm / Procedure

  • Use t.test() for different types of t-tests.
  • Interpret p-value and confidence intervals.

Input / Output

Input:
> t.test(x, y, paired=TRUE)
Output:
p-value < 0.05

Result

Applied t-test and interpreted results statistically.
